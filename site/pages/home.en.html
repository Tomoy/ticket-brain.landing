How it works

Four steps, and the second one is the point

Step 01

Take a photo of the receipt.

One photo, any supermarket. No typing, no connecting anything.

Step 02

Check what we read.

The AI extracts every line. You review it before anything is saved, so a bad read never slips into your budget without you noticing.

Step 03

See where the money went.

Spend by category, by supermarket, average per trip and month total.

Step 04

Get one concrete action.

"Bring Snacks & sweets back to your average and save €18.40 this month."

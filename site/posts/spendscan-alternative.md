If you are comparing grocery receipt scanners, SpendScan and TicketBrain will
both come up. They start from the same idea: your supermarket receipt already
contains a detailed record of your spending, and software should be able to
read it.

Where they differ is what they do with that data.

## What SpendScan emphasises

SpendScan describes itself as a grocery receipt scanner for tracking spending
and environmental impact, and its marketing leads with carbon footprint and
seasonal choices alongside the spending side. If reducing the environmental
impact of your shopping is a priority for you, that focus is a genuine
strength and worth taking seriously.

## What TicketBrain optimises for

TicketBrain is built around one question: **why does your grocery bill keep
growing, and what can you actually do about it?**

Every feature points at that. Automatic categorisation of each item, tracking
which categories are driving your budget up, comparing spending over time, and
surfacing specific actions based on your own shopping patterns rather than
generic advice.

Nutrition and environmental impact are on our roadmap, but we are deliberately
solving the money problem first and solving it well, instead of covering three
areas shallowly.

## How to choose

Pick SpendScan if environmental impact is the reason you want to scan
receipts in the first place.

Pick TicketBrain if your main problem is that your grocery spending has grown
and you cannot pin down where it went. That is the problem we are built for.

## An honest note on availability

TicketBrain is not launched yet. You can join the waitlist and be among the
first to use it, but if you need a receipt scanner working today, SpendScan is
shipping and we would rather tell you that than waste your time.

If the budget angle is what you care about, we would love to have you on the
list when we open up.

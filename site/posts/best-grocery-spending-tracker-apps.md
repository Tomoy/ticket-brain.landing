## Fetch: best if you want gift cards for your receipts

[Fetch](https://fetch.com/faq) is free to join and pays you in points for scanning grocery, shopping and restaurant receipts. You redeem points for gift cards, with most rewards starting at 3,000 points. According to its FAQ, you can scan up to 35 receipts in a rolling seven-day period, and paper receipts must be scanned within 14 days of purchase.

What Fetch doesn't do, at least according to its FAQ, is show you where your money goes. It describes using submitted receipts to help brands and retailers understand what people buy. Its [privacy policy](https://fetch.com/privacy-policy) says it may disclose your information to brand partners and advertising platforms, including details about products you've scanned or purchased.

**Pick Fetch if** you want something back for receipts you'd throw away anyway. We cover the trade-off in more detail in our [Fetch alternative](/blog/fetch-alternative) post.

## Ibotta: best for cash back on the brands you already buy

[Ibotta](https://help.ibotta.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008385000-How-does-Ibotta-work-A-Step-By-Step-Guide-for-the-App) is free on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id559887125). You add offers to your list before you shop, then earn cash back either by linking a store loyalty account or by uploading a photo of your receipt. The receipt is there to prove you bought the products on your list, not to analyze your spending.

On data, Ibotta's [help center](https://help.ibotta.com/hc/en-us/articles/223606587-Will-Ibotta-sell-my-contact-information) says it never sells or shares personally identifiable information like your name or email, but does share anonymized or de-identified information with third parties. You can opt out in the app's Preference Center.

**Pick Ibotta if** you plan your shop around specific product offers and want the cash back.

## YNAB: best for budgeting every dollar

[YNAB](https://www.ynab.com/pricing) (You Need A Budget) costs $14.99 a month or $109 a year, with a 34-day free trial. It's a full budgeting method, and it imports transactions directly from select US, Canadian, UK and EU banks. Its pricing page doesn't list receipt scanning.

Its [privacy policy](https://www.ynab.com/privacy-policy) states that it does not sell your financial data, and that bank connections go through third-party aggregators such as MX and Plaid, with your consent.

**Pick YNAB if** you want one system for rent, savings and groceries alike. The catch for groceries is that your bank only reports one total per shop. We explain why that matters in our [YNAB alternative for groceries](/blog/ynab-alternative-groceries) post.

## Monarch: best for seeing all your accounts together

[Monarch](https://www.monarch.com/pricing) costs $14.99 a month or $99.99 a year and syncs with your financial accounts. According to its [privacy policy](https://www.monarch.com/privacy), it connects through Plaid, Finicity, MX and Spinwheel and never receives your bank login. The policy says "we will never sell your financial data," while noting it may share profile or demographic data with advertising partners.

Like YNAB, its pricing page doesn't list receipt scanning, so your grocery spending shows up as one line per store visit.

**Pick Monarch if** you want net worth, investments and spending in one dashboard.

## SimplyWise: best for business receipts and taxes

[SimplyWise](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1538521095) describes itself on the App Store as a business tax report and expense tracker. Its AI receipt scanning captures the store name, date, tip, sales tax and total, and it can import receipts from email and accounts like Gmail, Amazon and PayPal. It's free to download, with in-app purchases ranging from $0.99 to $89.99, and documents are stored in its cloud.

It's built for expenses and write-offs, not for breaking down what's in your grocery cart.

**Pick SimplyWise if** you're self-employed and need receipts for taxes.

## SpendScan: a grocery receipt scanner with AI insights

[SpendScan](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6755533877) is the closest app to TicketBrain on this list. It scans grocery receipts with AI, picks out items and prices, sorts them into categories, and shows monthly spending by category, with a monthly spending limit you can set. It's free, with a Pro plan at $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year.

Its App Store privacy label lists contact info (email and name), photos and a user ID as data linked to you.

**Pick SpendScan if** it fits the way you shop. We compare it with TicketBrain in detail in our [SpendScan alternative](/blog/spendscan-alternative) post.

## TicketBrain: a grocery receipt scanner that keeps your data on your phone

[TicketBrain](/) is the app we build, for iPhone and Android, in English and Spanish. It does one job:

- **Scan a receipt** with your camera or from your photo gallery. The AI reads every line, with product, price and quantity, and gives it a category.
- **Review before saving.** You can fix any line, and the app warns you if a receipt looks like a duplicate.
- **Track your month.** The home screen shows your spending against your monthly budget and whether you're on pace. Insights break it down by category and by month.
- **Keep coupons from expiring.** Scan a coupon and get a reminder before it runs out.

The privacy model is the main difference. There's no account and no cloud: your receipts are stored on your phone, the receipt photo is processed once and not kept, and you can export a backup whenever you like. It's free during early access.

**What TicketBrain doesn't do:** it doesn't connect to your bank, doesn't pay you for receipts, doesn't compare prices between stores and doesn't make shopping lists. If any of those is what you need, one of the apps above is a better fit.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best free app to track grocery spending?

For seeing what's in each grocery bill, SpendScan has a free tier and TicketBrain is free during early access. Fetch and Ibotta are free too, but they're built to pay you for receipts and offers, not to track your spending.

### Is there an app that scans grocery receipts and tracks spending?

Yes. SpendScan and TicketBrain both scan grocery receipts, read each item and price, sort them into categories and show your monthly spending. SimplyWise scans receipts too, but it captures totals and tax for business expenses.

### Do Fetch or Ibotta track your grocery spending?

Not as their main purpose. Fetch turns receipts into points for gift cards and Ibotta uses them to confirm cash back offers. Neither describes a spending breakdown or budget in its help pages.

### Which budgeting apps connect to your bank?

YNAB and Monarch both connect to bank accounts through third-party services like Plaid and MX. They see one transaction per shop, not the items on the receipt. TicketBrain does not connect to your bank.

# Why TicketBrain Exists

In today's world, we're constantly spending money on groceries and everyday items, but do we really understand where our money goes? That's exactly why we created TicketBrain.

## The Problem We Solve

Most people have no clear picture of their supermarket spending patterns. They might know they spent $150 this week, but they don't know:

- Which categories consumed most of their budget
- How their spending compares to previous weeks
- Where they could save money without sacrificing quality
- What items they buy repeatedly vs. impulse purchases

## Our AI-Powered Solution

TicketBrain uses advanced artificial intelligence to:

1. **Automatically categorize** every item on your receipts
2. **Identify spending patterns** across weeks and months  
3. **Suggest smart savings** based on your actual purchasing behavior
4. **Track price changes** for items you buy regularly

## The Impact

Our users typically save 15-20% on their grocery bills within the first month, not by changing what they eat, but by understanding what they buy.
